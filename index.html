♜ 04 · How we work

Saying it twice doesn't make it true.

A claim climbs one rung only on fresh, independent observation. Repetition earns nothing.

  1. 01ATTESTED

    A worker says it happened. Unverified baseline.

  2. 02VERIFIED

    Independently checked against byte counts and hashes.

  3. 03LANDED

    Observed on the protected branch, CI green.

  4. 04HEALTHY

    Checked live after release, within bounds.
